# NIP-171: Private groups

The current draft is [Epoch-Ticketed Private Groups](nip171b.md).

It retains NIP-17 messages and signed epoch tickets, with owner recovery through a
12-word group master, deterministic epoch keys, encrypted recovery records, and
explicit handling of co-owner conflicts and master replacement.

The earlier Tiny Groups sketch has been superseded. No compatibility with it is required.
